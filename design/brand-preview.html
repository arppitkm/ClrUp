ClrUp  ·  Brand Concept

One idea carried through everything: a ring that closes as you reclaim space. The mark is a storage meter with a gap — the arrow lifts out through it. Mint means space you get back.

Mint is reserved for space reclaimed — nothing else is mint, so the number the user cares about is always the brightest thing on screen. Red appears only on the final confirm. Each category owns one accent, used consistently from dashboard ring to section header.
